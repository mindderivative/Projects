## Main Points
- Godot is a free and open-source game engine for creating video games.
- Particle systems in Godot are used to create visual effects such as smoke, fire, or explosions by manipulating a large number of small graphical objects called particles.
- The Godot particle system provides a variety of properties and settings to control the behavior of particle systems, including emitter properties, time properties, collision properties, drawing properties, and trail properties.
