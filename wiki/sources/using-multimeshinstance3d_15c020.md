## Core Content
MultiMeshInstance3D is a node in Godot Engine that creates multiple copies of a MeshInstance over a surface of a specific mesh. It is used to populate a surface with copies of a source mesh, such as placing trees on a landscape mesh. The basic setup requires three nodes: the MultiMeshInstance3D node and two MeshInstance3D nodes.
