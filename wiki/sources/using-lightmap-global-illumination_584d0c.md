## Core Content
Baked lightmaps are a technique for pre-calculating static lighting in a scene and storing it in textures. This workflow provides better performance and quality compared to real-time lighting calculations. However, baked lightmaps are not suitable for scenes with dynamic lighting or reflections.

## Main Points
- Baked lightmaps are a technique for pre-calculating static lighting in a scene and storing it in textures.
- Baked lightmaps provide better performance and quality compared to real-time lighting calculations.
- Baked lightmaps are not suitable for scenes with dynamic lighting or reflections.
- Reflection probes can be used to add reflections to scenes with baked lightmaps.
