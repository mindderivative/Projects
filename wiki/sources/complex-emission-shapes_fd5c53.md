# Complex Emission Shapes - Summary

## Source
- Original file: [[sources/using-the-surfacetool_7fe5f7|Using the SurfaceTool]]
- Ingested: 2026-06-30

## Core Content
In Godot's particle system, complex emission shapes allow users to generate particle emission points from arbitrary, complex shapes. These shapes are generated from meshes in the scene and stored as textures in the particle process material, enabling the creation of versatile particle effects that go beyond traditional use cases such as foliage, leaves on a tree, or holographic effects.
