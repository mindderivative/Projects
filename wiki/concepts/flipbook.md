# Flipbook

## Definition

A flipbook is a texture that contains several frames of animation that can be played back, or chosen at random during emission. It is used in particle systems to create more complex effects such as smoke, fire, and explosions. Flipbooks are equivalent to spritesheets for particles.

## Key Characteristics

- Contains several frames of animation

- Used in particle systems

- Creates complex effects like smoke, fire, and explosions

## Applications
