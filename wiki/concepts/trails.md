# Trails

## Definition
Trails is a feature in Godot game engine that allows users to add visual effects to particle systems. It involves creating and controlling the appearance and behavior of particle trails.

## Key Characteristics
- Adds visual effects to particle systems
- Controls the appearance and behavior of particle trails

## Applications
- Used in creating visually appealing effects in games and simulations
- Enhances the overall visual experience of particle systems
