# Display-referred

## Definition
Display-referred color spaces represent colors in terms of their values as they are displayed on a particular output device. It takes into account the specific transfer characteristics of the display.

## Key Characteristics
- Represents colors based on display values
- Accounts for the transfer characteristics of the specific display

## Applications
Display-referred color spaces are commonly used in computer graphics and digital imaging to ensure colors are accurately represented on various output devices.
