# Android devices support

## Definition
Android devices support refers to the compatibility of the Android editor with various Android devices. The editor requires devices running Android 5 Lollipop or higher, with at least OpenGL 3 support.

## Key Characteristics
- Compatibility with Android 5 Lollipop or higher
- OpenGL 3 support required

## Applications
The Android devices support feature ensures that the Android editor can be used on a wide range of devices, providing flexibility for users.
