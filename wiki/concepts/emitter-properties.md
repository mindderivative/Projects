# Emitter properties

## Definition
Emitter properties in Godot's particle system refer to the settings that control the behavior of a particle system, including the maximum number of particles visible at any given time, the ratio of particles compared to the amount that will be emitted, and the ability to spawn sub-emitters as child particles.

## Key Characteristics
- Maximum number of particles visible at any given time
- Ratio of particles compared to the amount that will be emitted
- Ability to spawn sub-emitters as child particles

## Applications
Emitter properties are used to control the visual output and performance of particle systems in Godot.
