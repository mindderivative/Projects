## Basic Information
Type: concept
Sources: [[sources/particle-turbulence_d714b0|particle-turbulence_d714b0]], [[sources/3d-particle-attractors_150d1c|3d-particle-attractors_150d1c]]
Definition: Particle attractors are nodes that apply a force to all particles within their reach. They pull particles closer or push them away based on the direction of that force.

## Description
Particle attractors are a technique used to control the movement of particles in a particle system. They are used in combination with turbulence to create more complex and interesting patterns. Particle attractors can be combined with collision nodes to create even more complex looking behavior. They provide a way to influence the movement of particles and control how they interact with each other and their environment.
