# Complex Emission Shapes

## Definition
Complex emission shapes are a technique in Godot's particle system that allows users to generate particle emission points from arbitrary, complex shapes.

## Key Characteristics
- Complex shapes are generated from meshes in the scene.
- The data for complex particle emission shapes is stored in a set of textures.

## Applications
- Create versatile particle effects beyond traditional use cases.
- Useful for creating foliage, leaves on a tree, or holographic effects.
