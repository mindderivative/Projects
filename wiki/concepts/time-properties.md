# Time properties

## Definition
Time properties in Godot's particle system refer to the settings that control the duration and timing of particle system behavior, including the lifetime of each particle, the interpolation towards the end of the lifetime, and the ability to preprocess the system to skip the initial setup time.

## Key Characteristics
- Controls the duration and timing of particle system behavior
- Includes settings for the lifetime of each particle
- Supports interpolation towards the end of the lifetime
- Allows preprocessing of the system to skip initial setup time

## Applications
- Adjusting the appearance and behavior of particles over time
- Optimizing particle systems by preprocessing them
- Creating realistic or stylized particle effects
