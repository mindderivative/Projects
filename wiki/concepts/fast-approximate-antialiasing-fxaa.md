# Fast Approximate Antialiasing (FXAA)

## Definition
Fast Approximate Antialiasing is a post-processing antialiasing solution that is faster to run than other techniques.

## Key Characteristics
- Effective at antialiasing transparency
- Lacks temporal information
- Does not do much against specular aliasing

## Applications
FXAA can be applied to various graphics applications, such as video games and visual effects, to improve image quality and performance.
