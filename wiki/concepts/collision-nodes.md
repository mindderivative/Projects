## Description
Collision nodes are a technique used in particle systems to control the movement of particles by defining collision rules and boundaries. The concept of collision shapes can also be applied to tilemap layers in the Godot engine. This allows collision shapes to be added to tiles in tilemap layers, providing functionality such as collision detection.
