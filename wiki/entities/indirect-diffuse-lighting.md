## Description
Indirect diffuse lighting is the lighting that does not change depending on the camera's angle. It is one of the key components of global illumination. There are two main sources of indirect diffuse lighting: light bouncing on surfaces and emissive materials that emit light and can be bounced on surfaces. Physical light units are a feature in Godot Engine that allow users to work with realistic lighting settings. They use real-world units for various properties related to light, such as color, energy, and intensity.
