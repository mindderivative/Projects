**Basic Information**

Turbulence is a tool for creating and manipulating procedural noise textures in Godot. It allows you to generate and modify noise patterns for use in various visual effects or as textures for 2D or 3D objects.

**Description**

Turbulence is an Entity in Godot that provides a wide range of noise textures, such as Perlin Noise, Simplex Noise, and Voronoi Noise, among others. These noise textures can be used to create natural effects like clouds, fire, water, and more. Turbulence allows users to tweak various parameters, such as frequency, amplitude, and octaves, to achieve the desired visual effect. You can also blend multiple noise types together to create even more intricate patterns. The textures generated by Turbulence can be used on materials for 2D sprites or 3D models, as well as for post-processing effects, like light scattering or screen space reflections.
