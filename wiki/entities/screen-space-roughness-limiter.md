# Screen-space Roughness Limiter

## Basic Information
- Type: concept
- Source: [[sources/3d-antialiasing_370351]]

## Description
Screen-space Roughness Limiter is a technique that reduces specular aliasing in 2D rendering by limiting the roughness of materials based on their normal maps. It works best on detailed geometry and has an effect on roughness map rendering itself, but its impact is limited there.
